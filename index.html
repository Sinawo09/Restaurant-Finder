A few helpful details

Questions, answered.

How do I find the right restaurant?

Browse by food category, or search the restaurant directory by name, food type, or city. You can also filter by location.

Do I have to share my location?

No. Finding restaurants near you is optional. If you allow location access, it is used to sort nearby results and is not saved or shared.

Can I book a table through Restaurant Finder?

Choose “View Details” and then “Book Now” to search for reservation options. Booking is completed with the restaurant or booking provider.
